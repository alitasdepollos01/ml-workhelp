How do updates work?

  • When there's a new version, your buddy tells you, and Settings › About & updates has a Download button.
  • Download the new .dmg (about 190 MB) and drag it over the old one in Applications. Your photos, stickers and work log stay.
  • Credit fixes and small updates arrive by themselves, with no reinstall. When there are new photos, your buddy asks before downloading them.

You may need to allow it again after each update. The app checks for a new version once a day by reading a small file on this page; nothing about you is sent. You can switch it off in Settings › About & updates.

Sizes in the panel
In the Explorer

A panel with a scene, and a Thai word on top

  • A mascot in a different scene each time: the office, a café, Wat Arun, Songkran, the fanmeet stage… 14 in all. It does little things there, and friends join in the group scenes.
  • The word card goes with the scene and shows how to say it. Click it for the next word, or tick ✓ I know this.
  • It sits in the Explorer next to your files, with your Pomodoro and today's work time underneath.
  • Sizes: Compact, Default or Big, or your own mix of buddy, friends, card and text.

The panel follows VS Code's light or dark theme.

On the editor's edges

One buddy walks round your editor

  • It walks the edges of the editor window, head pointing in: a gesture now and then, the odd dash, a sit-down, a nap while you're away and a hello when you're back.
  • Click it for a Thai word, drag it to any edge, right-click for its menu: a word, its special move, the Pomodoro, a break, a countdown, who walks, its size.
  • It reacts to your coding, with a Thai word to go with it. Each reaction can be turned off, and quiet hours keep the night calm.

Pomodoro, break pop-ups and reminders

  • Pomodoro: the classic 25 · 5 · 15, or your own lengths, counting down in the status bar; the buddy cheers when a round is done.
  • Next to the 🐾: how long you've worked since your last break. Five minutes away or a locked screen counts as a break.
  • Every 30–90 minutes of work a buddy pops up: Take a break, 10 more min, or Skip.
  • Reminders once, every day, on weekdays or every few minutes, each with a Thai word that goes with it; and a countdown.

Questions

Is it an app?

No, it's an extension. It lives inside VS Code and runs only while VS Code is open.

Does it need ML WorkHelp?

No. They're separate and don't share anything.

What about Windows?

So far it's tested on a Mac only. On Windows, the helper that lets the buddy walk outside the panel hasn't been tested yet, so we can't promise it works.

How do I remove it?

Extensions › Thai Mascot Buddy › Uninstall.

Privacy

No account, no sign-up, and nothing is sent online. To react to your coding it only notices things on your computer (a file saved, how many errors Problems shows, whether a test command passed, a commit); it doesn't read or keep your code.
