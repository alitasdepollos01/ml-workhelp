How it works

A day with ML WorkHelp

From the morning's first task to the end of the day: here's what it does, in the order you'll meet it.

Start your day, your way

Pick how you keep time at the top of Today, and switch with one tap; nothing is lost, and you can undo.

  • Work clock: counts while you're at the computer, task by task, and notices when you step away.
  • Pomodoro: focus rounds with breaks between; every round counts as today's work.
  • Set a focus of the day, and fix any part of today's timeline later.

A focus screen for work away from the keyboard

Reading, studying on paper, drawing? The clock fills the screen, and sitting still isn't counted as away. Try it here: change what's behind the clock and how the clock looks, just as in the app.

Reminders & events, just typed

Type “Stretch in 25 min” or “Fan meeting Sat 14:00–17:00” and the time is picked for you.

  • Works on its own, or in step both ways with Apple Reminders and Apple Calendar.
  • When it's time: a chime, or Milk and Love calling each other; the buddy holds it up for you.

Milk & Love, on your desktop

The desktop buddy walks along your screen, naps when you're away, and shows your clock underneath.

  • Gentle break reminders, and a MilkLove video (GMMTV OFFICIAL) on your break.
  • A Thai word now and then, with how to say it.
  • Choose who, which stickers, how big, and how often they change.

Your week, month and year

Three tabs, the same period:

  • Summary: the numbers with their trend, goal days, and each day by category (work, breaks or both).
  • Analysis: where the time went, when you work, your typical day, focus quality, side by side with before.
  • Calendar: click any day to see and change it. Make a picture of it, or export a CSV.
Your own stickers, credited

Add photos you're allowed to use; the people are cut out for you. Say who took each one, change it any time.

Help from Muv

Click Muv at the bottom left: answers, a short tour of each part, and a way to write to us.

Three languages

English, Traditional Chinese, and Thai (beta), everywhere in the app.

DevHelp · VS Code & Kiro

DevHelp: a light buddy while you code

An extension for VS Code and Kiro, small on purpose: GMMTV mascots and a few stickers keep you company, teach you Thai, keep your Pomodoro and remind you to rest. Here's each part.

DEVHELP

A tiny panel in the Explorer

  • A GMMTV mascot in a scene (the office, a café, Wat Arun, Songkran, the fanmeet stage… 14 in all), doing little activities, sometimes with friends.
  • A Thai word card on top, with how to say it: click it for the next word, or mark one you already know.
  • Underneath: your Pomodoro and today's work time.

Eight mascots, in cartoon or pixel style.

One buddy walks round your editor

  • Along the edges of the editor window, head pointing in: it pauses to show off a gesture, makes the odd dash, naps when you're away and hops when you're back.
  • Click it for a Thai word; pick it up and drop it on any edge.
  • A mascot, Milk & Love stickers, or the animals: you choose, and how big.

A desktop buddy, if you'd like one

  • Sits anywhere on your desktop, on any screen, even when the editor is away.
  • Does little activities (working, coffee, music, noodles…), and the line underneath counts your Pomodoro or today's work.
  • Holds up a Thai word now and then, and your reminders when it's time. Off until you switch it on.

Pomodoro, breaks and reminders

  • Pomodoro: classic 25 · 5 · 15, or your own lengths; the countdown sits in the status bar.
  • Without the Pomodoro, a work clock and a nudge to rest every 30–90 minutes.
  • At break time a buddy rises from the bottom of the editor: Take a break, 10 more min, or Skip.
  • Reminders once, every day, on weekdays or every few minutes, each with a Thai word that goes with it.

DevHelp keeps it light: no calendar, no data dashboards. For your whole work day, the calendar and seeing how you work over weeks and months, use ML WorkHelp.

Get DevHelp

DevHelp isn't in the extension store: you download one file and add it to VS Code or Kiro yourself. It takes a minute.

Download DevHelp 1.0.0 (.vsix)

One file for Mac and Windows · VS Code 1.85 or later, or Kiro.

  1. Open VS Code or Kiro, and go to Extensions (the squares on the left, or ⇧⌘X / Ctrl+Shift+X).
  2. Click … at the top of the Extensions list, then Install from VSIX…
  3. Pick the devhelp-1.0.0.vsix you downloaded (usually in Downloads).
  4. When it asks, press Reload. DevHelp's panel appears in the Explorer, and the buddy starts walking.

Or in a terminal: code --install-extension devhelp-1.0.0.vsix · kiro --install-extension devhelp-1.0.0.vsix

New versions don't install by themselves: leave your email below and we'll tell you, then install the new file the same way. To remove DevHelp: Extensions › DevHelp › Uninstall.

Versions

The app looks for a new version once a day by reading a small file on this page; nothing about you is sent. You can switch it off in Settings › About & updates.

Privacy

Your work log, reminders, photos and settings stay on your computer. There's no account, no tracking, and your work isn't sent anywhere. Apple Reminders and Calendar are only read and written on your own Mac.

The one thing that leaves your computer is the email you give when you first open the app: it goes to our mailing list (Buttondown), only so we can tell you about new versions and things you need to know. It's never shared, sold or used for anything else.
