ML WorkHelp Mac app

A MilkLove buddy for your work day

A work clock or the Pomodoro, a focus screen, reminders and events, stickers from your own photos, and your week, month and year in numbers, with Milk and Love keeping you company on screen.

FreeMac (Windows coming)Everything stays on your computerEnglish · 繁中 · ไทย (beta)

Step 1

Start your day, your way

Choose how you keep time at the top of Today, and switch with one tap. Nothing is lost, and you can undo.

  • Work clock: counts while you're at the computer, task by task, and notices when you step away.
  • Pomodoro: focus rounds with breaks in between; every round counts as today's work.
  • Set a focus of the day, and fix any part of today's timeline later.

Step 2

A focus screen for work away from the keyboard

Reading, studying on paper, drawing? The clock fills the screen, and sitting still isn't counted as away. Try it here: change what's behind the clock and how it looks, just like in the app.

Step 3

Reminders & events, just typed

Type “Stretch in 25 min” or “Fan meeting Sat 14:00–17:00” and the time is filled in for you.

  • Works on its own, or two-way with Apple Reminders and Apple Calendar.
  • When it's time: a chime, or Milk and Love calling each other's names, and the buddy holds up a sign for you.

Step 4

Milk & Love, on your desktop

  • The desktop buddy walks along your screen, naps when you're away, and shows your clock underneath.
  • Gentle break reminders, and a MilkLove video (GMMTV OFFICIAL) for your break.
  • A Thai word now and then, with how to say it.
  • Choose who appears, which stickers, how big, and how often they change.

Step 5

Make stickers from your own photos

Your stickers walk on your desktop, show up on the focus screen, and celebrate with you when you're done for the day. You choose which ones your buddy uses.

  1. Cut out: add a photo, and the people are cut out for you.
  2. Touch up: not quite right? Erase leftovers like a phone or a head in the crowd, Restore bits it missed, use Smart edges, or cut it by hand along a line you draw. Undo any time.
  3. Keep the one you like as a sticker, and say who took the photo. Click any sticker later to see its original photo and who took it.
Please respect the photographers

Only add photos you took yourself or have permission to use. Your stickers stay on your computer; the app never uploads or shares them. Stickers made in the app can only be used in the app.

Step 6

Your week, month and year

Three tabs, the same period:

  • Summary: the numbers with their trend, goal days, and each day by category.
  • Analysis: where the time went, when you work, your typical day and focus, side by side with before.
  • Calendar: click any day to see and change it; save a picture or export a CSV.

Thai Mascot Buddy VS Code extension

Thai words and a mascot buddy, right in VS Code

An extension you add to VS Code, not an app. A popular Thai mascot walks round your editor, teaches you a Thai word every few minutes, keeps your Pomodoro and tells you when to rest.

THAI MASCOT BUDDY

A panel with a scene, and a Thai word on top

  • A mascot in a different scene each time (the office, a café, Wat Arun, Songkran, the fanmeet stage… 14 in all), doing little activities, sometimes with friends.
  • A Thai word card that goes with the scene, with how to say it: click it for the next word, or mark the ones you already know.
  • It sits in the Explorer, next to your files, with your Pomodoro and today's work time underneath.

One buddy walks round your editor

  • Along the edges of the editor window, head pointing in: it shows off a gesture, makes the odd dash, naps when you're away and hops when you're back.
  • Click it for a Thai word; pick it up and drop it on any edge.
  • A mascot or the animals: you choose, and how big.

Pomodoro, break pop-ups and reminders

  • Pomodoro: the classic 25 · 5 · 15, or your own lengths; the countdown sits in the status bar.
  • Without the Pomodoro: your work time in the status bar, and a nudge to rest every 30–90 minutes.
  • At break time a buddy pops up: Take a break, 10 more min, or Skip.
  • Reminders once, every day, on weekdays or every few minutes, each with a Thai word that goes with it.

Pick the mascot you like

Eight popular Thai mascots, in cartoon or pixel style, or a friendly animal. Choose who walks round your editor, who pops up at break time, and how big they are.

Who the mascots belong to

The original mascots belong to GMMTV, Channel 3 and LingLing Kwong. We only redrew them, following the official art; we don't own them. If you're a rights holder and would like something changed or removed, please write to us.

Add it to VS Code in three steps

Nothing to download from this page: it comes from the VS Code Marketplace, and VS Code keeps it up to date.

Open Extensions

In VS Code, click the squares on the left, or press ⇧⌘X / Ctrl+Shift+X.

Search and install

Search for Thai Mascot Buddy and click Install.

Say hello

The panel appears in the Explorer, and the buddy starts walking.

Get it on VS Code Marketplace

Or type this in a terminal (it installs from the Marketplace too): code --install-extension alitasdepollos.thaimascotdevhelp

Needs VS Code 1.85 or later. Kiro: our Kiro version is in review.
